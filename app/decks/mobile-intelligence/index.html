Data is the moat in the AI era.

Your phone holds personal and work data. On-device SLMs put more control in your hands, with two ways to operate: local-first or cloud-first.

01 / Local-first

On my phone.

Keep my data with me. Use the cloud when I choose.

02 / Cloud-first

In the cloud.

Keep personal and work data in approved cloud spaces.

Your data. More choice over where intelligence works.

Proposed models. Cloud can also be private. Work data follows organisation policy; personal and work permissions stay separate.

The right intelligence is hybrid by design.

On the phone

  • Understand the immediate moment
  • Extract and classify information
  • Translate or simplify locally
  • Create a draft and detect missing context
  • Keep raw context private where possible

In the cloud

  • Retrieve authoritative information
  • Synchronise and collaborate
  • Apply organisation-wide policy
  • Execute workflows
  • Maintain official records and audit history

The phone is already where work happens.

The opportunity is to make it understand the work — privately, naturally and at the moment it matters.
